Most AI chat products give you one voice and a blank box. In All is All, the companion is the first thing you see, and I wanted people to decide who it is before they start talking. The feature is called the Archetype Forge, and its subtitle sums up the idea: design the interface of your connection.

## Four archetypes to start from

Writing a good persona is hard, so the Forge offers four guided archetypes, each with a one-line promise:

- **The Sage:** a non-judgmental, wise listener who offers perspective through metaphors and calm reflection.
- **The Stoic:** focuses on what we can control. Rational, grounded and emotionally resilient.
- **Old Friend:** casual, warm and deeply familiar.
- **The Void:** abstract and minimalist, for venting when you just want to hear echoes of your own thoughts.

The descriptions are written for the person choosing, not for the model. They describe how the conversation will feel.

## Or write your own

Under Manual Manifestation, two questions replace the prompt box: "Who are you representing?" and "Who should I become?" Asking for both sides matters. A companion that knows you are a tired founder speaks differently from one talking to a student before exams. Applying a new persona resets the chat, so the old voice never leaks into the new one.

## The instruction underneath

Behind the interface, every persona sits inside the same frame: be calm, reflective and supportive, use a gentle conversational tone, avoid clinical jargon unless asked, and stay in character if the person wants to role-play. The temperature is set to 0.7, warm enough to feel human, steady enough to stay on topic. Replies stream in word by word, which reads more like someone thinking than a page loading.

## Guardrails a calm product needs

A companion is not a therapist. All is All keeps a separate Therapy room that points people towards professionals. That separation is a design decision as much as an ethical one: the companion can be warm without pretending to be care.

If I designed this for wide release, I would add three things first: clear crisis resources that appear when certain topics come up, a visible note that replies come from an AI, and a one-tap way to clear the conversation from the device.

## What I took away

- **Let people set the relationship.** Choosing an archetype is a small act of control that changes how the whole conversation feels.
- **Write persona copy for humans.** "Focuses on what we can control" tells you more than a paragraph of prompt.
- **Reset on change.** A new persona deserves a clean start.
