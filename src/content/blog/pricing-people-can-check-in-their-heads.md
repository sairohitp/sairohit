Pricing is the part of a product page people read most carefully and trust least. Across three recent projects I ended up with the same rule: if someone cannot check the price in their head, they will not believe it.

## Dhobermans: one number first

Dhobermans leads with a single number: ₹100 per kg for wash, fold and iron, back within 24 hours, with free pickup and delivery within 5 km. Everything else builds on it.

- **Monthly packs** are 10% off: ₹90 per kg, so 20 kg is ₹1,800, 40 kg is ₹3,600 and 60 kg is ₹5,400. Anyone can verify those in a second.
- **Business plans** step down by volume: ₹85 per kg from 100 kg a month, ₹80 per kg from 250 kg.
- **Specialist services**, such as dry cleaning or curtains, are priced per item and confirmed on WhatsApp before pickup. That is stated plainly, rather than hidden behind a "from" price.

People do not know how much their laundry weighs, so the page includes a plan finder. You enter a typical week (T-shirts, trousers, towels, bedsheets) and it estimates the weight using everyday averages, such as about 200 g for a T-shirt and 800 g for a bedsheet. It turns a unit nobody thinks in into one they do.

## All is All: show the tax before the button

All is All's Pro plan costs ₹99 a month. At checkout, the summary shows the subtotal, the 18% GST of ₹17.82, and the total of ₹116.82, and the pay button repeats the exact total. The number on the button should never be a surprise.

## Progull: no price, said out loud

Progull is pre-revenue, so its pricing section says so plainly: there is no commercial price yet, and that is by design. There is a design-partner pilot at no charge, and a commercial plan marked "TBA" until those pilots close. The same page labels its headline figures as design targets, not measured results, and calls its simulations representative.

It sounds less impressive than a big number. It is also the only version an enterprise buyer will take seriously.

## The pattern

- **Lead with one number** people can remember.
- **Make discounts arithmetic,** not marketing.
- **Translate units** into things people already know.
- **Show tax and totals before the button.**
- **Label targets as targets.** Honesty is a feature.
